About

Everything about this extension and how to reach me.

JAMt

Version —

JAMt (Just Another Music Tally) keeps a running tally of everything you play on YouTube Music. It counts every track you listen to, tracks per-song and per-artist play counts, recognizes completed albums, and measures your listening time. Everything stays in your browser.
